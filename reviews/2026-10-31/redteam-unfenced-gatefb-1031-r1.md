1. CONFIRMED, medium. arena/factcheck.py:118 (ASIDE), 319 and 327 (a clause that names the extra keeps its price), 416-438. The bracket and dash cut is bypassed by writing the same wrong price with no mark. "The Vapor 17 Elite (with printing) is 400 EGP." fails, as round 3 asked, but these all pass: "The Vapor 17 Elite with printing is 400 EGP.", "... with name printing is 400 EGP.", "... with your name printed is 400 EGP.", "Vapor 17 Elite with printing costs 400 EGP", "Vapor 17 Elite with printing is 400 EGP only!", "الـ Vapor 17 Elite مع الطباعة بـ 400 جنيه", "الـ Vapor 17 Elite بالطباعة 400 جنيه بس", and every other aside shape: {with printing}, «with printing», curly quotes, -with printing- and the em dash with no spaces, / with printing /, | with printing |, full-width parentheses, ~ with printing ~. How found: ran factcheck.check on each (attack1.py B, attack2.py B). Why: the clause names the extra, so `own` holds 400 even though the same clause names the boot first and gives the boot's price. Impact: a wrong price counts as a won match and a passed rerun, so a lesson that teaches this way of quoting passes the gate. Fix (smallest): in the clause loop, when a clause names a boot or a tier and the extra, and the boot word comes before the extra word, leave the extra's price out of `own` unless `on_top` holds it. The extra's price then counts when the extra is the subject ("Printing on the Vapor 17 Elite is 400 EGP.", "The printing for the Vapor 17 Elite is 400 EGP.") or has its own clause ("The Phantom 6 Elite is 16,500 EGP, and the name printing is 400 EGP."). Checked against tests/test_price_clauses.py and test_round3_fixes.py: every passing line there has the extra first, a sum, or an add-on word, so none breaks.
