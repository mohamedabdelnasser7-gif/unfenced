Where: arena/run.py:222 (`p.is_symlink() or not p.is_file() or p.resolve().parent != folder.resolve()`) and arena/madeup.py:272 (`p.is_symlink() or not p.is_file() or ... st_size`).

### 2. A folder swapped between check_cards and load_cards passes both checks (low, UNCONFIRMED, a race)
