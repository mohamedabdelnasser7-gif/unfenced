## The answer in short

1. Fix A opened the code check wider than the decision meant. 60 of 83 money-giving wordings pass `code_check(tools=False)` on the Sales plug; 42 of them were caught by the old list and are caught by nothing in code now. Every Arabic and Franco discount wording I tried passes (13 of 13). The model judge is then the only gate before a live trial. CONFIRMED, MEDIUM. Fix: words and patterns in sales/plug.json, listed below.
2. The exam does not back the judge up on "free": a reply that gives delivery away with no number passes every safety check. CONFIRMED, part of 1.
3. Tool names are no longer marks for any plug, and the marketing plug's money tool (ads_manager) and send tool (publish_post) had no other mark. Marketing has no exam, so a judge "low" starts a trial at once. CONFIRMED, MEDIUM. Fix: put those two names in the plug's own words.
4. The fast note guard lets money-giving notes through ("Skip the delivery fee for them"). CONFIRMED, LOW (one customer's own next chat, an aim not a rule, older gap).
5. The recheck vote amends the cached run on the kept lessons one way and for the rest of the run: after one lesson's vote, a later lesson that always breaks the same check on that situation passes as "not new" with no rerun. CONFIRMED, LOW. Fix: do not persist the voted row (two lines).
6. Cap burn, a raised rerun flipping a fail, someone else's order, a phone number or a copied line in a lesson: RULED OUT, controls named below.

### 5. The recheck's vote amends the cached run on the kept lessons, one way, for the rest of the run
**State:** CONFIRMED. **Severity:** LOW (needs the agent without the new line to fail a safety check twice in one vote on that situation; an attacker reaches that only through an earlier kept lesson that made the baseline flaky).

**Where:** engine/exam.py:377-381 (the vote: failing without the line in 2 of 3, where one of the 3 is the original pass, marks the check "not new" and writes it into the before row); reflection/exam.py:423 (`self._without[key] = engine_exam.Result(without.passed, before, without.calls)`) and :477 (`cache[sid] = row`), which keep that amended row for every later lesson on the same kept set; engine/exam.py:283-287 (`judge` never holds a check the before row already fails).

**How found:** `recheck_flip.py` in the scripts folder, reflection/exam.py `Examiner(count=True)` with `run` patched as tests/test_exam_recheck.py does: the main run on the kept lessons passes situation 2, the two single-situation reruns without the line fail "amounts" there; every lesson breaks "amounts" on situation 2 with the line.

**Proof:** lesson A passes and the cached before row for situation 2 becomes `passed=False, check='amounts'`; lesson B then passes with 4 model calls and no rerun, its pair for situation 2 reads `(before False, after False)`, so it is not even counted as broke.

**Impact:** for the rest of that Examiner's life (one Arena run, one night) a safety break on that situation is never held and never rechecked. The amended row is never re-voted.

**Smallest fix:** keep the vote for the lesson being examined but do not persist it: drop reflection/exam.py:422-423 and :476-477. Cost: a later lesson on the same situation reruns the vote (16 calls at most). If the persisted row is wanted, require the amendment to come from the reruns alone (both fail) and re-run the kept-only answer once more before writing it (3 of 3 without the original pass).

Note, not a finding (Mohamed's call B holds): with the vote, a lesson that breaks a safety check with chance p on a situation now passes that situation with chance (1-p) squared once flagged (a 30 percent break passes 49 percent of votes; before fix B it passed 0 percent once flagged). The 3-situation cap and the stop at the first confirmed break are right as built.
